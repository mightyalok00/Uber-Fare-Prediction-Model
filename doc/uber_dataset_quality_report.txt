Important data-integrity finding
--------------------------------
The supplied distance_km does NOT correspond to the pickup/drop-off coordinates.
Correlation between stored distance_km and Haversine distance: 0.001563
Rows where stored and Haversine distance are within 0.1 km: 1.50%

By contrast, fare_amount is strongly related to stored distance_km:
Correlation between fare_amount and distance_km: 0.870630

Trip duration is also mechanically derived from stored distance_km:
trip_duration_min = distance_km * 3 for essentially every positive-distance row.

Therefore:
- Do not overwrite distance_km with Haversine distance.
- Do not claim the coordinates generated the stored distance.
- Treat this dataset as synthetic.
- For model training, use Completed trips.
- Keep distance_km as the source distance feature because it is the field actually related to fare.
- Use haversine_distance_km only as a data-quality/audit feature unless you retrain on a different, internally consistent dataset.
- passenger_count cannot be recovered from this dataset and should not be fabricated as real historical data.
